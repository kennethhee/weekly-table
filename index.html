The 8-Minute Sunday Glass-Bake Flow

Zero pots, zero frying pans, zero oil splatter. Raw ingredients go directly into your oven-safe glass meal prep containers.

01

Line Up Your 4 Glass Containers

Place 4 oven-safe glass containers on a baking tray. Divide 1/4 cup raw jasmine rice and 1/3 cup rinsed legumes (chickpeas, cannellini, or black beans) into each container. Add chopped tomatoes, mushrooms, or peppers.

02

Place Raw Chicken Cutlets on Top

Lay one raw 175g chicken breast cutlet directly on top of the grain bed in each container. Grains at the bottom; chicken resting above.

03

Pour Boiling Seasoned Bone Broth

In a kettle or small jug, mix 500ml boiling chicken bone broth with lemon juice, garlic, extra virgin olive oil, and herbs. Pour 1/2 cup of hot broth into each container over the chicken and grains.

04

Seal Tightly with Foil & Bake (35 Mins)

Crimp a sheet of aluminum foil tightly over the tops of the containers. Bake at 180°C fan-forced for 35 minutes. The boiling broth under the foil creates a sealed steam pocket, cooking the rice perfectly fluffy and keeping the lean chicken tender and juicy.

05

Uncover & Crisp (10 Mins)

Remove the foil and bake for a final 10 minutes until chicken is golden, cooked through to 74°C, and all liquid is absorbed.

06

Snap on Lids & Refrigerate

Cool for 15 minutes, snap on airtight container lids, and store in the fridge. 4 high-protein lunches ready for Monday through Thursday. Zero pots or pans to wash.
